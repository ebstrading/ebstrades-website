About EBS Trading LLC

A Dubai trading company built around one film brand.

We trade automotive films and accessories from Dubai, and we own the brand on every roll we sell.

Who we are

EBS Trading LLC is a UAE-based trading company for automotive films and accessories. Our flagship brand, Delta Wraps®, was established in 2012 and covers clear paint protection film, colour PPF and window film.

From our base in Dubai Industrial City we supply installers, detailing studios, dealers and distributors across the UAE, Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC. We also take enquiries from any market where a trade buyer needs a dependable film supplier.

Car owners can buy the same films by the roll from the Delta Wraps® online store at deltawraps.com.

How we work

Four commitments to every trade buyer.

Named materials

Elite PPF uses aliphatic TPU with raw materials from Lubrizol and Ashland. Colour PPF uses aliphatic TPU with Ashland adhesive. We say so in writing.

Published numbers

Thickness in mil and micron, VLT for every tint and warranty in years. You compare grades on data.

Written warranty

Films carry a 5 to 15-year warranty, depending on series and installation. Keep your purchase record to validate it.

Confirmed before you commit

Clear PPF and window film are in stock in the UAE. Colour PPF has a stated supply time, and shipping outside the UAE is confirmed before an order is placed.
