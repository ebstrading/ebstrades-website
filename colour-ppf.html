Colour PPF · Colour chart

More than 350 colours, supplied to order.

Colour PPF changes the colour of the car and protects the paint in one 7.5 mil TPU film. The first 100 colours of the range are shown here, grouped by colour family. Order any of them by its code.

Request a quote Colour PPF properties

How colour PPF is supplied

Made to order, with two delivery speeds.

Every customer wants a specific shade, so each roll is supplied to order in the exact colour chosen. Clear PPF and window film are different: those are always in stock in the UAE.

Screen colours are indicative. Metallic, pearl and colour-shift films change with the light, so confirm a shade against the physical swatch book before you order.
