Delta Wraps®. Designed to protect, perfected to perform.

The film brand of EBS Trading LLC since 2012. One name across clear paint protection film, colour PPF and window film.

Visit deltawraps.com Distribute Delta Wraps®

One brand, three film families.

Delta Wraps® was established in 2012 and is a registered trademark of EBS Trading LLC, registered with the UAE Ministry of Economy. The range is chosen for Gulf conditions: protection film that resists UV and yellowing, and nano ceramic tint that is fadeless.

The brand sells two ways. Trade buyers order by volume through EBS Trading LLC. Car owners buy by the roll from the Delta Wraps® online store, which also publishes guides on choosing film, caring for it and changing a car's colour legally in Dubai.

Films carry a 5 to 15-year warranty, depending on series and installation.
