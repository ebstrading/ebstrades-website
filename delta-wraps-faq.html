The brand

Who is behind Delta Wraps®.

What is Delta Wraps?

Delta Wraps® is a brand of automotive films: clear paint protection film (PPF), colour PPF and window film. It was established in 2012 and is owned by EBS Trading LLC, a trading company based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

Who owns Delta Wraps?

EBS Trading LLC owns the brand. Delta Wraps® is a registered trademark of EBS Trading LLC, registered with the UAE Ministry of Economy.

Where is Delta Wraps sold?

Car owners buy single rolls from the online store at deltawraps.com, priced in AED with delivery within the UAE. Installers, dealers and distributors buy by volume from EBS Trading LLC, which supplies the UAE, Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC and quotes other countries on request.

Is Delta Wraps film in stock in the UAE?

Clear PPF and window film are always in stock in the UAE. Colour PPF comes in more than 350 colours, so each roll is made to order in the shade the customer chooses.

Does EBS Trading ship Delta Wraps to Saudi Arabia and the rest of the GCC?

Yes, on request. EBS Trading LLC supplies trade buyers in Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain from the UAE, and quotes other countries case by case. Shipping is confirmed before the order is placed.

Clear paint protection film

Warranty, thickness and series.

What warranty does Delta Wraps PPF carry?

Elite clear PPF carries a 10-year warranty on every grade. Advance clear PPF carries 5 years at 6.5 mil and 7 years at 7.5 mil. Colour PPF carries up to 5 years with proper installation and maintenance. Keep the purchase record, because it is needed to validate a claim.

What is the difference between the Elite and Advance series?

Elite is aliphatic TPU made with raw materials from Lubrizol and Ashland. It comes in 7.5 mil gloss, 7.5 mil matte and 8.5 mil gloss, with a 10-year warranty. Advance is a flexible TPU film in 6.5 mil gloss, 7.5 mil gloss and 7.5 mil matte, with a 5 to 7-year warranty. Both are self-healing.

How thick is Delta Wraps PPF?

Clear PPF comes in 6.5 mil (165 µm), 7.5 mil (190 µm) and 8.5 mil (215 µm), depending on the grade. Colour PPF is 7.5 mil (190 µm).

Is Delta Wraps PPF self-healing?

Yes. Minor scratches and swirl marks in the top layer close up with heat, such as sunlight, warm water or controlled heat.

Does the film leave residue when it is removed?

The films are designed to come off without leaving residue. Colour PPF uses an Ashland automotive adhesive made for bubble-free application and residue-free removal.

What size is a roll of PPF?

Clear PPF and colour PPF come in one roll size, 1.52 m × 15 m.

Colour PPF

A colour change that also protects.

What is colour PPF?

Colour PPF is a 7.5 mil TPU paint protection film that carries colour. It changes the colour of the car and protects the paint underneath in one film.

How is colour PPF different from a vinyl wrap?

A vinyl wrap changes the colour. Colour PPF changes the colour and also takes the stone chips, at 7.5 mil of TPU. It heals minor scratches with heat, resists UV, yellowing, heat and humidity, and its adhesive is made to release without residue.

How many colours does Delta Wraps colour PPF come in?

More than 350, across gloss, matte, satin, metallic, colour-shift and carbon fibre pattern finishes. The first colours of the range are shown on the colour chart.

How long does a colour PPF order take?

Colour PPF is made to order. Urgent supply takes 6 to 8 days and normal supply takes 12 to 14 days for delivery in the UAE.

Window film

Series, shades and sizes.

Which window film series does Delta Wraps offer?

Three series in 25 shades between 1% and 78% VLT. TNC is titanium nano ceramic with a warranty of up to 15 years. NC is nano ceramic with up to 8 years. NCR is nano carbon with up to 5 years.

Which TNC shade matches the tint a customer asks for?

Customers in the GCC usually ask for tint by darkness, while rolls are labelled by VLT, the share of light let through. 80% to 90% tint is TNC-02, 70% to 80% is TNC-03, 50% to 70% is TNC-05, 40% to 50% is TNC-10, 30% to 40% is TNC-18, 20% to 30% is TNC-35, 15% to 20% is TNC-50 and 5% to 10% is TNC-70.

What sizes does window film come in?

NC and TNC window film come in one size, 1.52 m × 30 m. NCR window film comes in three widths, all 30 m long: 0.5 m, 1 m and 1.52 m.

Care

After the film is fitted.

How do you look after PPF after fitting?

Leave the film untouched for about 48 hours and handle it as little as possible in the first week. Wait 7 days before washing, then use pH-neutral shampoo and microfibre towels, and keep brushes and pressure jets away from the edges. Remove bird droppings promptly.

Who should fit the film?

Have the film fitted by an experienced, trained installer. Correct installation and care are part of the warranty conditions.
