Colour PPF

A colour change with the protection of PPF.

Made to order

With this many colours, every roll is supplied to order in the exact shade your customer chose. Pick urgent supply, 6 to 8 days, or normal supply, 12 to 14 days.

Why colour PPF instead of a vinyl wrap.

It protects as well as colours

A vinyl wrap changes the colour. Colour PPF changes the colour and takes the stone chips, at 7.5 mil of TPU.

It heals itself

Minor scratches and swirl marks close up with sunlight, warm water or controlled heat.

It holds up in the heat

The film resists UV, yellowing, heat and humidity, so the colour stays true in Gulf conditions.

It comes off clean

The adhesive is made to release without residue when the film is removed.

GCC tint guide · TNC series

Match the tint your customer asks for.

In the GCC, people describe tint by how dark it looks: 30%, 50%, 80%. Film rolls are labelled by VLT, which runs the other way. This guide matches the darkness a customer requests to the right TNC film.

Factory glass can carry a slight tint of its own, so the final shade varies by vehicle. Legal tint limits differ by country and vehicle type.

Care after fitting

What to tell the end customer.

These four points keep the film looking right and protect the warranty.

Use a trained installer

Have the film fitted by an experienced installer.

Leave it for 48 hours

Do not touch the film for about 48 hours, and handle it as little as possible in the first week.

Wait 7 days to wash

Then use pH-neutral shampoo and microfibre towels. Keep brushes and pressure jets away from the edges.

Clean marks early

Remove bird droppings promptly and dry the surface with a microfibre mitt.
