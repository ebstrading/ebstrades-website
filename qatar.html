Questions from buyers in Qatar

Before you send an enquiry.

Do you deliver to Doha, Al Rayyan and Al Wakrah?

We quote delivery to trade buyers anywhere in Qatar, including Doha, Al Rayyan and Al Wakrah. The route, cost and timing are confirmed in your quotation before you order.

Do you keep stock in Qatar?

No. Clear PPF and window film are held in stock in the UAE and ship to Qatar from there, typically after 2 to 5 business days of processing. Colour PPF is made to order.

Can I become a Delta Wraps® distributor in Qatar?

We welcome enquiries from distributors and resellers in Qatar. Send us your company details and the volumes you expect, and the sales team will reply.

What warranty do the films carry?

Elite PPF carries 10 years. Advance PPF carries 5 years at 6.5 mil and 7 years at 7.5 mil. Window film carries up to 15 years on TNC, up to 8 years on NC and up to 5 years on NCR.

Is there a minimum order for Qatar?

It depends on the product and the shipping route. Tell us what you need and we will confirm the quantities we can supply.
