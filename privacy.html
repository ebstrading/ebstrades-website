Privacy

Privacy notice.

How EBS Trading LLC handles the information you send us through this website.

What this website collects

This website does not store the details you type into the enquiry form. The form prepares a message on your own device. Nothing is sent to us until you choose to send that message by WhatsApp or email.

What we do with your enquiry

When you send an enquiry, we receive the details you included, such as your name, company, country, email address and phone number. We use them to reply to you, prepare a quotation and process any order that follows.

We do not sell your details. We share them only where it is needed to fulfil your order, for example with a courier or freight company.

Other services

Messages sent by WhatsApp or email are handled by those services under their own privacy terms. Links on this site to deltawraps.com and to social media take you to sites with their own privacy notices. This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete them, by writing to info@ebstrades.com.
