Inside a clear paint protection filmDelta Wraps® Elite grades measure 7.5 or 8.5 mil in total (190 or 215 µm).

  1. ASelf-healing top coat. Light scratches and swirl marks close up with heat.
  2. BAliphatic TPU film. The body of the film. It takes the stone chips so the paint does not.
  3. CAdhesive. Holds the film to the panel and comes away without residue.
  4. DRelease liner. Protects the adhesive on the roll and is peeled off at installation.

Window film shades

From 1% to 78% visible light transmission.

VLT is the share of visible light a film lets through. A lower number means a darker film. Pick a series to see its shades.

In the GCC, customers ask for tint by how dark it looks, not by VLT. See the GCC tint guide to match the two.

Legal tint limits differ by country and vehicle type. Check the limit in your market before you order dark shades.

How an order runs

Four steps from enquiry to delivery.

  1. Send your enquiry

    Tell us the products, quantities and destination country. WhatsApp and email both reach the sales team.

  2. Receive a quotation

    We confirm grades, availability, price and shipping. International shipping is always confirmed before you place the order.

  3. We prepare the order

    Standard orders are typically processed in 2 to 5 business days. Colour PPF can take longer, and we confirm the lead time first.
